# Design System: Zunix Night Cut

## Visual theme
A dark cinematic portfolio with the energy of an RGB editing suite. Density 4, variance 9, motion 8. Asymmetric editorial structure keeps the work readable while coral, cyan, and acid light create a more expressive visual signature.

## Palette
- Night Canvas `#08080D`: site background
- Raised Black `#11111A`: media frames and cards
- Soft White `#F5F3FF`: primary text
- Muted Lavender Gray `#A19EAF`: descriptions and metadata
- Signal Pink `#FF4D8D`: primary actions
- Electric Cyan `#38E8FF`: metadata and secondary light
- Acid Lime `#DFFF4F`: rare atmospheric highlights

## Typography
Archivo is the wide grotesk display and body family. IBM Plex Mono handles metadata. Headlines use tight tracking and wide measures. Body copy uses relaxed leading and stays below 65 characters per line.

## Components
Buttons use a compact rectangular 10px radius, clear contrast, and tactile press feedback. Media surfaces use a consistent 14px radius. Cards appear only for meaningful grouped media. Form labels sit above inputs and errors stay inline.

## Layout
Content is contained at 1400px. Heroes use an asymmetric split. Project layouts vary in proportion and avoid equal three-column rows. All grids collapse to one column on small screens with 44px touch targets.

## Motion
Reveal transitions animate opacity and transform only. Project images scale subtly inside clipped frames. Reduced-motion preferences disable all nonessential animation.

## Banned patterns
No pure black, generic purple-blue AI gradients, theme switching, centered hero, equal landscape and portrait ratios, fake dashboards, emoji, filler scroll cues, or horizontal mobile overflow.
